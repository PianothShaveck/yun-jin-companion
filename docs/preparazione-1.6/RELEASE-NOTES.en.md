# Yun Jin Companion 1.6 — Unreleased

- The calendar image viewer now confirms when the original is displayed and distinguishes local files, cached images and new downloads.
- If the original cannot be downloaded or decoded, the preview remains visible with a clear status and a retry button.
- Original calendar images are decoded in the background while the preview remains visible. Closing the viewer discards the pending result; only one original is decoded at a time.
