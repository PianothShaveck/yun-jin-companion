# Sonno: fonti, prompt e sequenze finali

Generazione tramite lo strumento imagegen integrato, con sfondo trasparente. Riferimenti: sprite originale `app/spritesheet-yun-jin-v2.png` e sequenza approvata `app/assets/conduct-16.png`. Nessuna animazione precedente è stata modificata.

## Addormentamento - prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: 8-frame sprite sheet for the existing Yun Jin desktop companion, new 'falling asleep' animation. Input 1 is the original exact identity, chibi proportions and palette reference (standing, running and falling frames). Input 2 is a recent approved rendering for linework/colors. Generate a TRANSPARENT PNG horizontal sheet, exactly 4 columns x 2 rows of equal square cells, ideally 2048x1024. Exactly eight full-body sprites, row-major order, one per cell, no labels or grid. Maintain IDENTICAL scale across all frames, ample transparent separation. Neutral standing height 420px per512cell; crown head width ~170px, original small shoes, proportionate body/arms, no huge baby head. Yun Jin's dark violet flowing blackish hair is separate from the TWO SLIM CYAN BRAIDED headdress cords with gold flower fastenings and cyan tassels. Preserve her mauve pink sleeves/cape, dark purple embroidered short dress, white frilled hem, pink tights, small dark boots, elaborate headdress with pink center ball and cyan/white balls, wine-red eyes. Exact muted cyan, rose pink and plum color palette of inputs, thin soft linework, polished 2D shaded sprite, never bold outlines. All clothing remains modest and complete. She gently gets sleepy then settles down on the floor, kneeling/sitting with legs tucked beside her and head resting on folded arms over her knees, unmistakably peacefully asleep, eyes closed. Continuous carefully staged physical descent, no morphing or squashing anatomy: frame1 standing neutral, frame2 drooping eyelids and small yawn with hand near mouth, frame3 ending yawn knees softly bending, frame4 crouching with hands lowered, frame5 sitting with legs tucked to one side head still up, frame6 elbows on knees hands together head lowering, frame7 eyes closed cheek resting on folded hands and knees, frame8 same final sleeping pose settling gently. Broad physical movement comes from joints and bending legs, NOT shrinking the character. Hat and head retain same physical size throughout. Hair and cyan cords fall naturally and remain distinct. Shared floor baseline around92% cellheight; last sprite lower overall because seated, NOT scaled down. No props, cushions, bed, text, letters, shadows extending out of cell, duplicate limbs, cropped feet, smeared hands, frame overlap, stray marks, thick contour, motion streaks. The final pose must be reusable as the starting pose of a slow sleep loop.

Sorgente selezionata: `source-art/sleep-enter.png`.

## Respiro - prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create 4-frame SLEEP LOOP sprite atlas for Yun Jin desktop companion, a continuation of the first input's LAST sprite at lower right. First image is exact identity/style and sleeping pose reference; second is original color/proportions reference. Transparent background, 2 columns x 2 rows, four completely separate full-body sleeping sprites, no grid or text. Each sprite should faithfully match the FINAL LOWER-RIGHT figure of input1: sitting on floor with small dark boots tucked in front, knees drawn up, head leaning slightly toward viewer left and resting on two folded hands over knees, eyes completely closed, hair and skirt resting naturally to sides. Keep the very same head/hat size relative to body, identical thin cyan cords with gold flowers separate from dark violet hair, mauve pink fabric, gray-plum patterned dress, white hem. Four close but genuinely distinct drawings of an extremely slow breathing loop: frame1 rest after exhale, frame2 halfway inhaling head/shoulders rise just 1%, frame3 full inhale chest/head rise2% and hair/cord tips subtly settle, frame4 halfway exhaling returns toward frame1. Absolutely no gesture, no new props, no waking, no waving, no open eyes, no Zs, no text, no grid, no bold outlines, no head enlargement, no dress/hat detail redesign. Delicate smooth 2D shaded sprite, wine-red eyes not visible while closed. Each frame same physical scale and baseline, full silhouette fits inside its cell with 12% transparent padding horizontally and ample padding vertically. Preserve alpha. Continuous clean cycle with maximum tiny breathing variation, NOT 4 unrelated poses.

Sorgente selezionata: `source-art/sleep-breathing.png`.

## Risveglio e montaggio

Una generazione separata del risveglio è stata scartata perché introduceva differenze nelle proporzioni testa/corpo. Il risveglio finale usa le otto pose approvate dell’addormentamento in ordine inverso, con durate dedicate. Sono tre clip e tre stati distinti, senza salto di disegno alla giunzione.

`tools/prepare_sleep_animations.py` applica soltanto ritaglio e impaginazione delle figure isolate e una scala uniforme al ciclo di respiro, senza deformazioni. Produce 8, 6 e 8 frame di riproduzione; i sei del respiro comprendono quattro disegni nuovi e la posa di raccordo alle estremità. Il manifest conserva una singola scala per l’intera sequenza e lo stesso punto d’appoggio.

Dopo la revisione: `tools/calibrate_animation_palette.py --review CARTELLA --source-assets TAVOLE_NON_CORRETTE --names sleep_in sleep_loop sleep_out --output docs/palette-calibration-sleep.json`; poi `tools/color_match_animations.py --source-assets TAVOLE_NON_CORRETTE --config docs/palette-calibration-sleep.json`. Il filtro non altera alpha o geometria; gli hash impediscono applicazioni ripetute.
